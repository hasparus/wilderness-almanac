# Major Arcana artwork

Artwork by **YY Game**, from the purchased
[Pixel Tarot Cards - Major Arcana Asset Pack](https://yygameca.itch.io/monochrome-pixel-tarot-cards-major-arcana-asset-pack).

The 22 PNGs retain their original 47 × 97 pixels and have only been renamed to
match the Party Journal card names. The matching card back is not used.
